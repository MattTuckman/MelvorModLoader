Merchant 0.1.0  -  a mod for Melvor Idle 2          (in development, not released yet)
======================================================================================

A travelling merchant with a page of his own in the game, next to the Bank and the Shop.

   For sale            raw materials you are already able to gather (ores, logs, raw fish),
                       for 2 times what the bank pays; one bargain and two rare finds a visit
   He is looking for   three goods he buys for 1.5 times the bank price
   Your order          choose one raw material and he always has it with him
   New goods           every 4 hours of real time

Buying something and selling it back to him never earns gold, and what he is looking for
(and anything it is made of) he does not sell on the same visit.

Tested with game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 and Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3.


REQUIRES
--------
Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3 or newer. Install the loader first.


HOW TO USE
----------
Open a character. The Merchant button is in the top bar, between Bank and Shop; F3 opens
the page as well, and takes you back to where you were.
A small dot on the button: gold when he has new goods you have not looked at, green when
something he is looking for is in your bank.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- The page is built of the game's own panels, letterings and buttons, taken from your
  installed game while it runs. No picture of the game is shipped with the mod.
- What you bought and sold on a visit, and your order, are kept per character in
  "merchant.json" next to the mod. Nothing is written into your save.
- He never sells or buys a stack you have locked in the bank.


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete the folder "mods\Merchant".
