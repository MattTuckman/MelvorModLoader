COMBAT ADVISOR 0.1.0  -  for Melvor Idle 2 (requires Melvor Mod Loader)
=======================================================================

Before you fight: is it safe to leave running, how fast you kill each monster, and
the XP and food per hour. Worked out from YOUR gear, stance, food, levels and every
bonus you have right now (prayers, potions, pets, upgrades...).

WHERE
Open the game's combat area menu (Combat Areas, Slayer Zones...). Every monster card
gets a line at the bottom:

    SAFE   533 kills/h   3.0k Strength XP/h   4.3 food/h            [Advisor]

"Advisor" opens the full window, in the order you would ask:
1. Is it safe?  SAFE / RISKY / DEADLY with one sentence, and a gauge: its biggest
   hit as a bar from 0, against your auto-eat point (gold line) and your max HP
   (white line). Stops before the gold line: safe. Crosses it: risky. Crosses the
   white line too: deadly. When it is not safe, it says what would make it safe.
2. What you fight with: weapon, special attack, food, and "Stance:" buttons that
   preview another stance (only the numbers change, never your stance in the game;
   "Back to ..." returns).
3. What you get per hour: kills, XP for every skill your stance trains, and
   supplies (food, ammunition, runes) for 1 or 8 hours, with "+20% spare" for
   unlucky streaks, and how long what you have lasts.
4. "Show details": hit chances, damage, timing, the special attack in words.
5. The other monsters of the area side by side, "Best safe XP" marked. Click one
   to see it.
Esc or Close closes the window (the game's area menu stays open).

THE VERDICTS
SAFE     no single hit can take you from just above your auto-eat point to 0.
RISKY    a hit just above your auto-eat point can take you down, you have no food
         for a monster that hurts you, you cannot hurt it fast enough, or its
         special attacks knocked you out in the test kills.
DEADLY   one hit can do more than all your HP.
UNKNOWN  a mechanic the advisor cannot work out yet (it says which).
"Safe" is not a promise against bad luck. The advisor never shows a death chance.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Nothing in your game is changed: the fights are worked out on a private copy of
  your bonuses. Nothing is written into your save.
- Monsters with special attacks or effects are played out 2,000 kills hit by hit,
  on a thread of their own: their line fills in a moment later.
- Your own weapon's special attack (Dragonrune Greatsword's Crushing Blade, a bow's
  multi-shot...) is counted too, hit by hit: its extra hits, stuns, bleeding and
  poison on the monster, the boosts it gives you, ammunition for every shot. The
  card says "Crushing Blade counted". If some part of it is not counted, the card
  says "partly counted" and the details name it.
- Your XP bonuses and the chance to keep ammunition are counted.
- Dungeons are not forecast yet (their wave cards get no line).
- Checked against the game's own fights: kills, XP, damage taken, food and ammunition
  per kill match real fights within a few percent.

INSTALL
Unzip into the game folder (where Melvor2.exe is). The mod ends up in
mods\CombatAdvisor. Remove that folder to uninstall.
