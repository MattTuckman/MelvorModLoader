[center][size=6][b]ACTION QUEUE[/b][/size]
[size=4][i]Do this, then that, then that.[/i][/size]
Line up actions with a goal each and let the game work through them.
Not a cheat: it only presses the buttons you would press, at the moment you would press them.[/center]

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Action Queue [b]0.2.2[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.6-alpha.1[/b] with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. Open a character and press [b]F6[/b].

[size=5][b]An example[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Woodcutting: Oak Tree, [i]until 1,000 Oak Log in the bank[/i]
[*]Firemaking: Oak Log, [i]until it stops by itself[/i] (the logs are used up)
[*]Fishing: Shallow Pools, [i]until Fishing level 30[/i]
[*]Combat: Golbin, [i]for 45 minutes[/i]
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Pick what to do:[/b] choose a skill and an action from the lists, or press "Use what I am doing now".
[*][b]Pick when it ends:[/b] when it stops by itself, when you own a number of an item, when a skill reaches a level, or after a number of minutes. For the item goal the item is filled in with what the action makes (Smithing: Gold Bar counts Gold Bars); you can still pick another one.
[*][b]Start the queue.[/b] One line at the top of the screen shows the current step and its progress.
[*][b]Start over at the end[/b] turns the queue into a loop.
[/list]

[size=5][b]16 skills[/b][/size]
Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Firemaking, Cooking, Smithing, Crafting, Runecrafting, Fletching, Beastcrafting, Herblore, Thieving, Agility, Astrology, Hunting and Combat (a monster, a dungeon or a slayer master's tasks).

[b]Slayer tasks as a step:[/b] "Slayer tasks of <master>" fights whatever that master's task is and walks to the new monster when a task is done. Unlike the game's own Auto Slayer switch it can have a goal, for example "until Slayer level 20", and then hand over to the next step.

[size=5][b]It plays fair and it plays safe[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Never bypasses requirements.[/b] A step you are not allowed to do yet, or have no materials for, is marked "skipped" with the reason, and the next one starts.
[*][b]You stay in charge.[/b] Start something else by hand and the queue pauses instead of fighting you.
[*][b]Death pauses it.[/b] If your character dies the queue stops and does not walk back into the fight, even with "start over" switched on.
[*][b]Runs while the game is open.[/b] While the game is closed, the game's normal offline progress continues the last action only.
[*]Hunting needs a trap and Agility needs a path chosen on the skill's own page first. The queue does not make that choice for you.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Melvor Mod Loader[/b] first (see Requirements).
[*]Open the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Extract [b]ActionQueue-0.2.2.zip[/b] there. The mod ends up in [b]mods\ActionQueue[/b].
[*]Start the game from Steam as usual.
[/list]
No installer, no .exe, no script. Nothing is written outside the game folder.
