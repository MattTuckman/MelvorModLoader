MOD ADI (Name):
Action Queue

KATEGORI ONERISI (Category):
Gameplay

KISA ACIKLAMA (Summary / Brief overview):
Chain actions in Melvor Idle 2: cut 1,000 logs, burn them, fish to level 30, then fight. Or name an item ("100 Steel Platebodies") and the planner works out every step. 16 skills, never bypasses requirements. Not a cheat.

[center][size=6][b]ACTION QUEUE[/b][/size]
[size=4][i]Do this, then that, then that.[/i][/size]
Line up actions with a goal each and let the game work through them.
Not a cheat: it only presses the buttons you would press, at the moment you would press them.[/center]

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Action Queue [b]0.4.0[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.6-alpha.1[/b] with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. Open a character and press [b]F6[/b].

[size=5][b]An example[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Woodcutting: Oak Tree, [i]until 1,000 Oak Log in the bank[/i]
[*]Firemaking: Oak Log, [i]until it stops by itself[/i] (the logs are used up)
[*]Fishing: Shallow Pools, [i]until Fishing level 30[/i]
[*]Combat: Golbin, [i]for 45 minutes[/i]
[/list]

[size=5][b]New: the planner[/b][/size]
Press [b]Plan an item[/b], choose [b]Make[/b] (this many more) or [b]Own in total[/b], type the number and pick the item. For "100 Air Wands" you get:
[list=1]
[*]Mining: Rune Essence, [i]until 1,776 in the bank[/i]
[*]Smithing: Molten Glass, [i]until 294[/i]
[*]Crafting: Unpowered Orb, [i]until 100[/i]
[*]Runecrafting: Air Rune, [i]until 1,200[/i]
[*]...and so on, down to the wands themselves
[/list]
[list]
[*][b]It follows the recipes for you:[/b] armour needs bars, bars need ores. Every amount is worked out with your character's current bonuses.
[*][b]It counts your bank:[/b] what you already own is taken off.
[*][b]It tells you how long:[/b] each step shows the time it takes at normal game speed. In testing the forecast was within a few percent for most skills.
[*][b]One click adds it:[/b] the steps go to the end of the queue as ordinary steps. Reorder them, remove them, mix them with your own.
[*][b]Plans stack:[/b] add a second and a third plan. A later plan knows what the earlier ones will make and use up, so two plans never count on the same ore.
[*][b]It is honest about gaps:[/b] actions you have not unlocked yet are shown in red, and items no skill makes (monster drops, shop items) are listed in yellow for you to bring.
[/list]
Plans cover Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Hunting, Smithing, Cooking, Crafting, Runecrafting, Fletching, Beastcrafting, Herblore and Firemaking: more than 1,000 items.

[size=5][b]New: saved queues and share codes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Keep several queues.[/b] "Smithing day", "Fishing loop", "Combat grind": save each under a name.
[*][b]Switch in one click.[/b] The list at the top of the window loads a saved queue. A running queue keeps running, from the first step of the new one.
[*][b]Nothing is lost by switching.[/b] A queue you never saved, or changed, is kept as "Unsaved queue (auto)".
[*][b]Share a queue.[/b] "Copy share code" turns it into a short text like [font=Courier New]AQ1:q1byU7JSc...[/font]. Paste it in Discord. Anyone can paste it into the box and press Import.
[*][b]Codes are safe to take.[/b] A code holds only names and numbers. Steps for things your game does not have, or that you have not unlocked, are simply skipped.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Pick what to do:[/b] choose a skill and an action from the lists, or press "Use what I am doing now".
[*][b]Pick when it ends:[/b] when it stops by itself, when you own a number of an item, when a skill reaches a level, after a number of minutes, or when only a number of an item are left (fight until only 5 of your food are left). For the item goal the item is filled in with what the action makes (Smithing: Gold Bar counts Gold Bars); you can still pick another one.
[*][b]Start the queue.[/b] One line at the top of the screen shows the current step and its progress.
[*][b]Start over at the end[/b] turns the queue into a loop.
[/list]

[size=5][b]16 skills[/b][/size]
Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Firemaking, Cooking, Smithing, Crafting, Runecrafting, Fletching, Beastcrafting, Herblore, Thieving, Agility, Astrology, Hunting and Combat (a monster, a dungeon or a slayer master's tasks).

[b]Slayer tasks as a step:[/b] "Slayer tasks of <master>" fights whatever that master's task is and walks to the new monster when a task is done. Unlike the game's own Auto Slayer switch it can have a goal, for example "until Slayer level 20", and then hand over to the next step.

[size=5][b]Works with Multitask[/b][/size]
With the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/8][b]Multitask[/b][/url] mod (same author) you can keep extra actions running in its slots while the queue works through your main action step by step.

[size=5][b]It plays fair and it plays safe[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Never bypasses requirements.[/b] A step you are not allowed to do yet, or have no materials for, is marked "skipped" with the reason, and the next one starts.
[*][b]You stay in charge.[/b] Start something else by hand and the queue pauses instead of fighting you.
[*][b]Death pauses it.[/b] If your character dies the queue stops and does not walk back into the fight, even with "start over" switched on.
[*][b]Runs while the game is open.[/b] While the game is closed, the game's normal offline progress continues the last action only.
[*]Hunting needs a trap and Agility needs a path chosen on the skill's own page first. The queue does not make that choice for you.
[/list]

[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Melvor Mod Loader[/b] first (see Requirements).
[*]Open the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Extract [b]ActionQueue-0.4.0.zip[/b] there. The mod ends up in [b]mods\ActionQueue[/b].
[*]Start the game from Steam as usual.
