Bulk Sell - Bank Buttons

KATEGORI ONERISI (Category):
User interface

KISA ACIKLAMA (Summary / Brief overview, 228 karakter):
Adds buttons to the bank of Melvor Idle 2: a Bulk sell window with filters and "keep N of each", one-click Sell junk, Bury all and Open all. Uses the game's own prices and functions. Locked stacks are never touched. Not a cheat.

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Bulk Sell [b]0.2.1[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.6-alpha.1[/b] with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. Open the bank: the new buttons sit next to the search box. [b]F7[/b] opens the bulk sell window from anywhere.

[size=5][b]Bulk sell window[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every sellable stack in one table: icon, amount, price each, what you would get.
[*]Click a row to mark it. "All shown", "None" and "Invert shown" mark many at once.
[*]Filter by search text, by bank tab and by kind (Woodcutting, Smithing, ... or "No skill uses it").
[*][b]Keep of each:[/b] leave a number of every marked item in the bank.
[*]Click a column title to sort, for example most valuable first.
[*]The bottom line always shows how many items go and for how much. Selling needs a second click.
[/list]

[size=5][b]One-click buttons in the bank[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Sell junk:[/b] mark the things you never want once, save them as your junk list, and from then on one button sells whatever of it is in the bank.
[*][b]Bury all:[/b] buries every bone and other buriable item. Only shown when there is something to bury.
[*][b]Open all:[/b] opens every chest, bag and pack. Only shown when there is something to open.
[/list]
Each of these asks once before it acts: the button text turns into a question such as "Sell 229,484 for 1,360,052 Gold Pieces?" and only a second click does it.

[size=5][b]Your locks are respected[/b][/size]
A stack you have locked in the bank never appears in the list and is never sold, buried or opened.

The game already lets you select several stacks with Ctrl and Shift. This mod adds what that lacks: filters, "keep N of each", a junk list and the one-click buttons.
